Problem 102: Simply supported beam carrying a uniform process load. Span length L = 3.66 m with a rectangular section 190 mm by 304 mm fabricated from aluminum 6082-T6 extrusion. The beam is supporting a mezzanine tool bay with a pinned support at the west end and a roller at the east end. Apply a uniformly distributed factored load of 9.95 kN/m along the entire span. Use Young's modulus E = 210 GPa and Poisson ratio nu = 0.30. Determine the midspan deflection and extreme-fiber bending stress.
