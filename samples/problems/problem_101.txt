Problem 101: Simply supported beam carrying a uniform process load. Span length L = 3.61 m with a rectangular section 178 mm by 320 mm fabricated from laminated southern pine. The beam is carrying an energy-storage rack with a pinned support at the west end and a roller at the east end. Apply a uniformly distributed factored load of 9.25 kN/m along the entire span. Use Young's modulus E = 205 GPa and Poisson ratio nu = 0.30. Determine the midspan deflection and extreme-fiber bending stress.
